# Rewrite the USA 401(k) grant website

## Goal
Update every page so the program is consistently described as a no-cost grant for eligible working U.S. citizens who own a 401(k), with awards based on a review of each applicant's information.

## Changes
- Rewrite the home page headline, overview, process, examples, calls to action, and footer.
- Rewrite eligibility requirements and the interactive checker around the stated rules: working U.S. citizen, age 18+, and 401(k) ownership.
- Clearly state that the grant is separate from the applicant's 401(k), does not withdraw or reduce its balance, has no fees, and requires no repayment.
- Explain that approved funds may be used for business needs, personal needs, or financial problems, and that the final amount depends on the application review.
- Update the application wording and fields so applicants describe employment, intended use, and financial needs rather than being required to own or start a business.
- Update dashboard and About page wording to match the same program description.
- Remove unsupported fixed award ranges, approval statistics, invented legislation, agency partnerships, fabricated leadership, testimonials, and other claims not supplied in the description.
- Preserve the existing visual design, navigation, and working interactions.

## Validation
- Search all pages and scripts for contradictory old wording.
- Open each page in the preview and verify the updated text and eligibility checker render without errors.
