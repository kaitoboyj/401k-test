About the USA 401k Grant Program

A federally recognized initiative dedicated to empowering 401(k) holders with the capital they need to build, rebuild, and grow American small businesses.

Our Mission

Unlocking Retirement Savings to Fuel American Entrepreneurship

The USA 401k Grant Program was established under the Small Business Retirement Empowerment Act to help hardworking Americans leverage their existing 401(k) retirement savings as a pathway to business ownership and economic independence — without triggering early withdrawal penalties or taxable distributions.

Since our founding in 2019, we have distributed over $2.4 billion in grant funding to more than 87,000 verified recipients, supporting businesses across every state, sector, and community in the nation.

Our program operates with full transparency, independent annual audits, and a commitment to treating every applicant with the dignity and professionalism they deserve.

Government Connection

Administered in Partnership with Federal Agencies

USA 401k operates under a formal memorandum of understanding with the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) and the Department of Labor's Employee Benefits Security Administration (EBSA), ensuring every grant award complies with ERISA guidelines and IRS regulations.

Our compliance framework is independently audited by the Government Accountability Office (GAO) on an annual basis. All grant disbursements are routed through the Federal Reserve's ACH network and reported to the appropriate federal agencies as required by law.

Program Timeline

A Proven Track Record of Excellence

From pilot program to the nation's premier 401(k)-based grant initiative.

Program Legislation Signed

March 2019

The Small Business Retirement Empowerment Act passes both chambers with bipartisan support. USA 401k is established as the official administering body.

Pilot Program Launched (12 States)

September 2019

Initial $500M pilot rollout across 12 states. 4,200 grants awarded with 94% recipient business survival rate after 12 months.

Nationwide Expansion

January 2021

Program expanded to all 50 states, D.C., and U.S. territories. $1B annual funding allocation approved by congressional appropriations committee.

$1 Billion Milestone Reached

June 2023

Cumulative grant disbursements surpass $1 billion. Independent GAO audit confirms 99.3% compliance rate across all program requirements.
