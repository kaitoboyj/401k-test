Are You Eligible for Up to $40,000?

Review our official requirements below or use our interactive 2-minute checker to see if you qualify instantly.

Official Program Requirements

Active 401(k) Account

You must hold a current, active 401(k) retirement account with a registered U.S. financial institution. Rollover IRAs from former 401(k) plans also qualify.

Minimum Account Balance of $10,000

Your 401(k) must have a balance of at least $10,000. Higher balances generally correspond to larger grant awards, up to the $40,000 maximum.

Business Ownership or Startup Intent

Grants are for business purposes only. You must either own an existing small business or have a concrete plan to launch one within 90 days of receiving funds.

U.S. Citizenship or Legal Residency

Applicants must be U.S. citizens, permanent residents (green card holders), or hold valid work-authorized visa status and a Social Security number.

18 Years of Age or Older

All applicants must be legally adult individuals with the authority to enter binding agreements and conduct financial transactions independently.

Valid U.S. Bank Account

Approved grants are disbursed via ACH transfer. You must have a personal or business checking account with a U.S.-based FDIC-insured institution.

How Your Grant Size is Determined

Your final award is calculated using a transparent, weighted formula based on the following official factors.

40%

401(k) Account Balance

The largest single factor. Accounts over $150,000 receive the highest consideration; minimum qualifying balance is $10,000.

25%

Business Viability Score

Your business plan summary, industry sector, and projected 12-month revenue impact are evaluated by our review team.

20%

Financial Responsibility

Credit profile, existing debt load, and history of on-time obligations are reviewed to ensure prudent stewardship of grant funds.

15%

Economic Impact Factor

Businesses located in Opportunity Zones, underserved communities, or creating W-2 jobs receive boosted grant consideration.
