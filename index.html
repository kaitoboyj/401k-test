How It Works

A Clear Four-Step Application Process

Eligibility and award amounts are determined after the information in each application has been reviewed.

1

Confirm 401(k) Ownership

Provide information that confirms you own a 401(k). This is only an eligibility check; no money is withdrawn from your retirement account.

2

Tell Us About Your Needs

Share your personal and employment information and explain whether the grant would support a business, a personal need, or a financial problem.

3

Get Reviewed by Our Team

Our review team considers the information you provide to determine whether you are approved and the amount you may receive.

4

Receive Your Grant

If approved, you receive the awarded amount without application charges or repayment. Your 401(k) balance remains unchanged.
