How It Works

Your Path to Up to $40,000 in 4 Simple Steps

Our streamlined process ensures qualified 401(k) holders receive their grant quickly, securely, and with complete transparency.

1

Connect & Verify Your 401(k)

Securely link your 401(k) account through our bank-level encrypted portal. We verify account status and balance with your provider instantly.

2

Submit Simple Application

Complete our 5-minute application form with basic personal details and your intended business use for the grant funds.

3

Get Reviewed by Our Team

Our licensed compliance team reviews your submission. 98% of eligible applicants receive conditional approval within 48 hours.

4

Receive Your Grant

Once approved, funds are deposited directly into your designated bank account via ACH transfer, typically within 7 business days.
